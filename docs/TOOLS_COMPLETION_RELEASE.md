# DreamForgeX tool completion release

This candidate implements presentation creation, timeline editing and export, custom model training and use, and image animation. Production release requires the wallet and media prerequisites in [WALLET_ACCOUNTING_RELEASE.md](WALLET_ACCOUNTING_RELEASE.md) and [MEDIA_REVOCATION_RELEASE.md](MEDIA_REVOCATION_RELEASE.md), the new migrations, and signed-in provider acceptance.

## Implemented behavior

| Tool            | User flow                                                                                                                                                             | Persistence and output                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Presentations   | Generate structured slide content, edit titles, body and speaker notes, then generate selected slide images with a displayed quote.                                   | Drafts restore within the same account and browser tab. Editable JSON supports backup and import. PDF export includes images, wrapped content, continued pages and notes. PDF text is rasterized to preserve multilingual glyphs.                                                                           |
| Timeline        | Create `/timeline/new` or edit an authorized project; upload media, import owned generations, arrange and trim clips, edit text and audio levels, preview and export. | Project saves preserve existing project data and use revision checks. Recording runs in real time and requires the tab to stay visible. |
| Custom models   | Agency users upload 5–20 images, prepare a dataset, review the quote, start training, refresh or request cancellation, then use or download a completed model.        | Three saved model slots. Durable jobs retain provider receipts and credit provenance. Downloaded weights, inputs and generated custom images use private media access. Model deletion removes unshared training inputs and weights.                                                                         |
| Image animation | Upload an image or select an owned completed image in `/tools/image-to-video` or the workspace. Choose or describe motion and review the quote.                       | A real five-second 480p MP4 is copied to owned storage and saved in generation history. The former `video.imageToVideo` API refuses unquoted requests before charging. Clients must use `animation.importSource`, `animation.start` and `animation.status`. The older workspace `generation.animateImage` entry also refuses legacy calls and directs clients to the revised tool.

## Credit policy

Presentation drafting costs 5 credits; slide images use the selected executable image model's quote. PDF and timeline export do not charge generation credits. Training costs 10,000 credits for 800 steps or 15,000 for 1,200 steps. Custom-model image generation costs 210 credits per started megapixel, with dimensions limited to 256–1,024 in multiples of eight. A 1,024-square image costs 420 credits. Animation costs 1,293 credits for five seconds at 480p.

The training and animation prices follow the existing credit margin policy and the verified [Fal trainer pricing](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-lora-fast-training), [custom-model image pricing](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-lora), and [Wan image-to-video pricing](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/wan-25-preview/image-to-video). Provider pricing must be checked again before release. Configuration or eligibility refusals happen before reservation. Confirmed failures refund the original purchased-credit portion once. Ambiguous submissions retain their request identity and require reconciliation; they never automatically invoke a second paid job.

Known provider receipts can be recovered by status checks or `/api/cron/stale-generations`, including after the browser closes. Each cron invocation bounds recovery to one training job, one custom image and two animations. Recovery polls and copies existing results; it does not submit paid jobs. An accepted cancellation request is not reported as a completed cancellation or refund.

## Migration and configuration order

1. Review the existing wallet opening balances, historical annual service periods, media ownership inventory and cache purge prerequisites.
2. Apply the unchanged migration chain through 0016, then 0017 for training ownership and private classification, followed by 0018 for timeline projects and bounded upload sessions. Journal timestamps must remain strictly increasing. A real Drizzle rehearsal verifies fresh installation, upgrade from 0017 and repeat execution.
3. Verify Fal credentials for the trainer, custom image model and animation model; existing text-generation configuration; R2 read/write/delete/signing access; and `CRON_SECRET`. Private storage requires a distinct `R2_PRIVATE_BUCKET_NAME`, separate `R2_PRIVATE_ACCESS_KEY_ID` and `R2_PRIVATE_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, and a scoped `R2_PRIVATE_READ_TOKEN` that can inspect the bucket’s Cloudflare domain configuration. The application verifies that the managed public domain is disabled and no enabled custom domain exists before private storage access; successful checks are cached for at most 30 seconds, and failed or missing verification denies reads, writes and signing. Deletion can still remove revoked bytes during a verification outage. It never falls back to the public bucket. `/img` refuses registered private keys. Historical public objects and cached copies retain the separate media-release requirements.
4. Deploy the reviewed application candidate with the project DRI's sign-off. Confirm the existing stale-generation and media-cleanup schedules are enabled and monitor reconciliation and object-deletion queues.

Uploads use same-origin chunks of at most 3 MiB. Images are limited to 10 MiB, audio to 20 MiB and video to 25 MiB; training and animation accept images only. Completion validates size, hashes and decoded type. Private playback supports byte ranges. Expired uploads tombstone every expected key before deleting their upload session, so a late PUT cannot restore access.

## Acceptance before production

The complete local suite passes 1,434 tests with six environment-dependent tests skipped. Local verification covers credit races and rollback, purchased-credit refunds, ownership, collaborator revocation, revision conflicts, uploads, private reads, account deletion, actual provider protocol fixtures, durable receipts and migration order. The exported nine-page PDF has been rendered and visually inspected, including CJK, Arabic, Greek, emoji, images, long content and continued notes.

Visible Chrome timeline export passes against the application renderer: a requested 0.8-second clip produces a playable 0.845-second VP8/Opus WebM at 1,280 × 720. Decoded frames confirm the trimmed blue source, moving foreground and title; decoded audio confirms the mixed tone; cancellation returns `AbortError` without offering a partial download. Independent ffprobe inspection confirms the video/audio streams and 27 decoded video frames. The local `timeline-browser-qa.html` harness reproduces the test without paid calls.

A bounded signed-in acceptance run must verify one presentation, slide image, completed training job, custom-model image and animation against the configured providers, including result recovery after leaving the page. Use synthetic, authorized fixtures and a separately approved spending limit. This candidate does not establish live provider credential validity or authorize production migrations, reconciliation, deployment or paid acceptance calls.
